The orchard at Fenwick Lane had a good year [EM] better than anyone expected after the dry spring.
Pickers arrived before dawn, and by noon the first crates were stacked beside the old scale house.

Nobody spoke much about the weather. It was enough to see the ladders leaning in every row, the
baskets filling, and the lanterns hung ready for the evening shift [EM] a quiet, unhurried kind of
work that the town has done for as long as anyone can remember.
