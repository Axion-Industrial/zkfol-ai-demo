# Fenwick Orchard Cooperative: company overview

Fenwick Orchard Cooperative is a fictional fruit grower and cider maker used for this demo.
All names, figures and addresses in these documents are invented.

The cooperative has 42 member farms across three valleys. Its main products are eating
apples, pears and a range of ciders. It employs 118 people at peak harvest and 64 for the
rest of the year. The head office is at 14 Fenwick Lane, Millbrook.
