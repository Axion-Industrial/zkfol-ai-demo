* V0.4.0
** Features
- Guards compile: ~x > 2~ reaches the predicate as a committed slack row
- The lay is a value: link-and-lay yields ~Zkfol.Lay~, the witness its
  matrix; the pipeline runs Doubling, Witness, Lang, and evaluation
  never lowers
- ~Zkfol.eval~ answers without proofs; ~Zkfol.Query~ steps answers
  like prolog's ~;~, ~statement/2~ fills a prove, ~stream~ is the
  lazy spelling
- ~mod/2~ is a relation: a quotient region and a slack bound; ~Facts~
  reads the modulus off the clause, the doubled walk runs Z_m, and
  the kernel is written in the sugar
- The scope is carried: a relation knows its home; compile takes the
  root alone
- The journal names the fired clause: Consumption deleted, and a free
  ask derives without the ground replay
- GT: query views (~;~, findall, click-to-statement); a forward hides
  when its target cannot feed
** Changes
- Examples, docs and comments cut to what earns its place
- The lay speaks index-form: the Lang pass at 10k columns, 28s to 0.2s
- ~Matrix~ drops unproduced constructors; the refusal index closes
- Call outputs desugar like inputs; ~_~ is anonymous per occurrence
** Dependencies
- AL to the 0.2.1 preview, carrying our six accepted fixes
- ~gt_bridge~ 0.19.4
- Zinc+ pntt wall at 2^20 codewords
* V0.3.0
** Features
- ~Al.solve~ searches in any direction: bind any rows, leave any free
- Declared guards: ~x > 2~ as a goal steers the derivation (not yet
  the emitted predicate)
- Negative literals in the surface
- Composed reads prove natively: the value-addressed statement lands
  on Zinc+'s pointer query instead of waiting at the prover boundary,
  and the accumulator fallback it replaced goes
- Closures: several relations run as one chain, each column wearing
  its relation's tag; a forged fact fails the judgement, and a call
  may target what an earlier call answered
- ~Face.judgement~ and the GT judgement suite: heatmap, column and
  cell drill-downs, round-trippable sources
- Row provenance: a ~%Uair{}~ names the row each committed column
  carries, and the grid labels lead with it
** Changes
- Arguments address the relation's own rows, root first then scope
- ~Semantics.valid?/2~ is the judgement's one door, total over any
  interpretation
- ~Al.apply~, ~Al.question/1~ and ~program/1~ round out the AL
  surface; refusals are typed, and nothing searches by witness size
- The unproduced go: the BitPoly ~Lookup~ mode and ~Zkfol.Range~,
  each returning the day an emitter produces one
** Dependencies
- Zinc+ bumps to the fork's pointer query, pinned at 7cf72c4
- ~gt_bridge~ 0.19.2
* V0.2.0
** Features
- The relational surface: defrel writes a relation as clauses over
  the core language, compiled to the same IR the handwritten
  predicates produced; the handwritten predicates are gone
- The witness oracle unified: interpretation, semantics and range
  live together, and witnesses are derived from the statement,
  never restated
- Typed refusals: a pass that declines says why with a value
- The order-2 descriptor: Facts reads a recurrence off a relation,
  and the doubling pass consumes descriptors
- The compiler core: Zkfol.compile evaluates a Statement through
  the pipeline stages; passes implement one behaviour (run/2,
  plan/2); a journaled act leaves a Ran receipt whose trail is a
  query over the log and whose stages are pure re-runs
- The face: Zkfol.Face shapes statements, grids, routes and runs
  for a viewer; every feed is a pure function of a log snapshot
- One examples book: Examples.EUser defines the corpus with defrel
  and threads default arguments; everything derivable is derived
- Glamorous Toolkit views declared Elixir side over gt_bridge
  0.19.1: the pipeline route as a mondrian, its passes as a list,
  and a Pass tab on the module coder reading journaled runs off
  the log
- AL support: FOL expressions evaluate on AL, and the witness is
  obtained from it
- The review skill stands alone; formatter and dialyzer settings
  ride the docs topic

* V0.1.0
** Features
- Replaced python codebase with an Elixir one
- The paper's logic (eprint 2024/954) as a typed AST: Figure 1 terms
  over one matrix, composed cells as pointers, reify, len
- Figure 3 as the single oracle: Semantics.eval judges terms and
  predicates over the integers, and every witness answers to it
- Statements carry ranges and claims; interpretations are the
  matrices they judge
- Zinc+ driven in process over a Rustler NIF, pinned to the
  mariari/zinc-plus fork; padded traces wall at 8192 rows under the
  pinned PnttConfig, three cell widths
- Uair emission of canonical constraint programs, sizes held by
  regression gates: 279 rows for the doubled kernel, 111 generic
- The prover as an actor behind a broker; proving journals to the
  log at the backend boundary
- A pipeline of passes over statements, the trace a value
- The doubling compiler: order-2 recurrences rewritten to kernels
  logarithmic in n; fibonacci at 10,000 is 6 rows by 14 columns,
  claiming the full value; ~EBench~ records the milliseconds
- Witness generation from seeds, every column oracle judged
- The registers layout: the zkVM loop as a statement
- Examples are the tests, composed with ex_example; every number
  claimed here is asserted in Examples.EBench
